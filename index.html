Bubble Sort

Repeatedly compares adjacent values and swaps them when they are in the wrong order. Large values gradually “bubble” to the right.

Best: O(n)Average: O(n²)Worst: O(n²)Space: O(1)

How to read the animation

Bars represent values: taller bars mean larger numbers. Yellow marks values being compared, red marks movement, purple marks a pivot or insertion key, and green marks values confirmed in their final sorted positions. Drag the timeline under the bars to scrub through the sort.
